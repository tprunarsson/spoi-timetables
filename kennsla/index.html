Fyrstu skrefGetting started

0. Fyrstu skref0. Getting started

The viewer can open Tímabókanir, find the courses that need attention, knows the parts of the screen, and knows how saving works.

BókunBooking

1a. Að lesa dagatalið1a. Reading the calendar

The viewer can tell booked, unbooked and unscheduled sessions apart, and read the clash pills and the status of a session's current position.

1b. Að laga það sem vantar1b. Fixing what's missing

The viewer can pair a session with its booking, give an unscheduled session a time, and create and delete sessions.

1c. Að breyta og bóka1c. Changing and booking

The viewer can move a session, read what the move does (clashes before → after), change its room, length, type, note and weeks, and knows what Bóka does and when it is blocked.

StillingarSettings

2a. Stillingar tíma2a. A session's settings

The viewer knows what each of a session's settings does - Heiti, Nemendafjöldi, Virkt, Tímasetja, Úthluta stofu, Samhliða - and sees Samhliða change the clash pills.

2b. Óskir fyrir Besta2b. Wishes for Besta

The viewer can set wished times (green, yellow, red) and rooms (green, yellow, or removed), and knows how Besta uses each.

2c. Víðværar stillingar2c. Course-wide settings

The viewer can mark two courses as co-taught and sees that they then never clash and may share a room.

Kennarar og óskirTeachers and their wishes

3a. Kennarar námskeiðs3a. A course's teachers

The viewer can see who teaches which session, record a teacher's wishes (times, weeks, rooms, a note) and save them with Afgreiða óskir.

3b. Könnun kennara3b. The teachers' survey

The viewer knows what a teacher sees and does on the survey page, and what each answer means.

3c. Úr könnun í Spoi3c. From survey to Spoi

The viewer can set up the survey, fetch the answers, find the courses with open answers and process them into the sessions.

Stundatöflur og BestaTimetables and Besta

4a. Að opna stundatöflu4a. Opening a timetable

The viewer can find or create a stundatafla (a named set of courses, e.g. one programme's term), add courses, and read the calendar, the summary and the session table.

4b. Breytt í höndunum4b. Changing by hand

The viewer can move a session, read the clash preview while dragging, change its room, pin its time, remove a session or a course, and save with Vista.

4c. Besta4c. Besta

The viewer can run Besta on a stundatafla, knows what may move and what counts, compares the proposals with Núverandi lausn, adjusts one and adopts it with Vista.

4d. Birting og bókun4d. Sharing and booking

The viewer can publish a stundatafla on the web, copy or delete one, open a teacher's timetable, and knows where booking in TermTime will happen.
